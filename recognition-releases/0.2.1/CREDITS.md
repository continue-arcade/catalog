# 来源与署名 / Sources and credits

## Yinks 图鉴识别资料 / Yinks recognition data

游戏名称、编号、指纹和分类来自同批冻结的 Yinks 公开图鉴与经审核的补充识别资料。公开图鉴保留 IGDB、No-Intro、Redump、Pocket Museum、RetroAchievements 等来源的编号。本站整理的识别资料仅摘取已准许分发的事实字段；游戏、商标及各来源目录的权利仍归原权利人。精确的公开版本、快照摘要和补充资料摘要记录于随包清单。

Titles, identifiers, fingerprints and classification references come from the frozen Yinks publication and its approved recognition evidence. Source identifiers retained by Yinks include IGDB, No-Intro, Redump, Pocket Museum and RetroAchievements. The Cart distributes factual recognition fields. Rights in the games, trademarks and original catalogs remain with their respective owners. The bundled manifest records the publication version and exact input digests.

## 街机标准文件名 / Arcade set names

文件名与作品标题整理自 MAME 的五份驱动登记，经 Continue 的 Catalog 审核后用于离线识别。作品标题与商标归各自权利人所有。卡带仅分发识别资料和展示图，不包含 ROM、BIOS 或密钥。

Known filenames and game titles are derived from five MAME driver registrations and reviewed in the Continue Catalog. Game titles and trademarks belong to their respective owners. This Cart contains recognition data and presentation images.
